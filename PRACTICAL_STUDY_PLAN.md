### Step 2

Memorise these numbers first:

- 50 km/h
- 60 km/h
- 80 km/h
- 100 km/h
- 120 km/h
- 5 metres
- 15 metres before / 5 metres after
- 45 cm
- 1.6 mm
- 2 seconds / 4 seconds

### Step 3

Say the answers out loud.

Do not just read them silently.

### Step 4

Do one proper pre-test lesson and ask the instructor to drill:

- the 47 questions
- road signs
- secondary controls
- technical checks

## 5-Day Reset

### Day 1

- Read the RSA practical-test page and booklet
- Read questions 1 to 15

### Day 2

- Read questions 16 to 30
- Learn the sign and markings shortlist

### Day 3

- Read questions 31 to 47
- Do the technical-check prompts

### Day 4

- Mixed oral recall without notes
- One mock run with a friend or instructor

### Day 5

- Pre-test lesson
- Light review only afterwards
