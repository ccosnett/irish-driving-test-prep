# Road Signs Shortlist

These are the practical-test signs and markings to know cold.

## Sign Families

- Warning signs: yellow diamond
- Regulatory signs: circular
- Motorway signs: blue
- National road signs: green
- Regional and local road signs: white with black text

## Must-Know Signs

- Stop sign
- Yield sign
- No entry sign
- Roundabout sign
- Speed-limit signs: 50, 60, 80, 100, 120
- School warden stop sign

## Must-Know Markings

- Broken white centre line
- Continuous white centre line
- Double broken white lines
- Continuous plus broken white line: obey the nearest line
- Broken yellow side line: hard shoulder
- Single yellow line: no parking at stated times
- Double yellow lines: no parking at any time
- Yellow box junction
- Zebra crossing zig-zag lines
- No-entry road marking

## Must-Know Crossings And Lights

- Pelican crossing
- Zebra crossing
- Flashing amber light
- Flashing red railway lights
- Filter arrow

## Best Official Source

- [Rules of the Road PDF](https://www.rsa.ie/docs/default-source/road-safety/rules-of-the-road.pdf)
