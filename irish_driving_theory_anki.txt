What is the national speed limit on a single carriageway in Ireland?	80 km/h
What is the speed limit on a motorway in Ireland?	120 km/h
What is the speed limit in a built-up area (town/city)?	50 km/h
Who has right of way at a roundabout?	Traffic already on the roundabout
What does a broken white line in the centre of the road mean?	You may overtake if it is safe to do so
What does a continuous white line in the centre of the road mean?	You must not overtake or cross the line
At what distance from a junction is it illegal to park?	5 metres
What is the minimum tyre tread depth required by law?	1.6 mm
What is the legal blood alcohol limit for a learner driver in Ireland?	20 mg per 100 ml of blood (effectively zero)
What is the legal blood alcohol limit for a fully licensed driver?	50 mg per 100 ml of blood
What colour are motorway signs in Ireland?	Blue
What colour are national road signs?	Green
What colour are regional road signs?	White
What does a flashing amber traffic light mean?	Proceed with caution — give way to pedestrians
What should you do if an amber traffic light shows steady (not flashing)?	Stop, unless it is unsafe to do so
What is the penalty points system threshold for disqualification (full licence)?	12 points in a 3-year period
What is the penalty points threshold for disqualification for a learner permit holder?	7 points in a 3-year period
How far from a pedestrian crossing is it illegal to park?	15 metres before and 5 metres after
When must you use dipped headlights?	At night in built-up areas, in fog, heavy rain, or when meeting oncoming traffic
What does a yellow box junction mean?	Do not enter unless you can clear the junction (exception: turning right if your exit is clear)
What shape is a warning sign in Ireland?	Diamond (yellow background, black border)
What shape is a regulatory sign?	Circular
What should you do at a stop sign?	Come to a complete stop and yield to traffic
What should you do at a yield sign?	Give way to traffic on the road you are joining; stop if necessary
What is aquaplaning?	When a layer of water builds between the tyres and the road, causing loss of control
What is the safe following distance rule?	The two-second rule (four seconds in wet conditions)
When can you use the hard shoulder of a motorway?	Only in an emergency
What is the minimum age for a learner permit for a car (Category B)?	17 years old
As a learner driver, what must you display on the vehicle?	L plates (front and rear)
Must a learner driver be accompanied?	Yes — by a qualified driver who has held a full licence for at least 2 years
What does a broken yellow line along the edge of the road mean?	No parking during certain hours (check signs)
What does a double yellow line along the edge of the road mean?	No parking at any time
When should you use your horn?	Only to warn other road users of danger — never between 11:30 PM and 7:00 AM in a built-up area
What should you do if you break down on a motorway?	Pull onto the hard shoulder, switch on hazard lights, use the emergency phone, do NOT attempt repairs yourself
What is the sequence of traffic lights in Ireland?	Green → Amber → Red → Red+Amber → Green
What must you do before opening your car door?	Check mirrors and look for cyclists/pedestrians
What does the sign "Yield right of way" (inverted triangle) require you to do?	Give way to traffic on the major road
In wet conditions, how does braking distance change?	It at least doubles
What is the purpose of an NCT (National Car Test)?	To check the roadworthiness of vehicles — required for cars over 4 years old
What lane should you normally drive in on a motorway?	The left-hand (inside) lane, unless overtaking
What should you do if you see a school warden holding a stop sign?	Stop and wait until all children have crossed and the warden signals you to proceed
What is the legal requirement regarding seatbelts?	All occupants must wear seatbelts — the driver is responsible for passengers under 17
At a junction with no signs or signals, who has priority?	Traffic on the major road; if roads are equal, traffic from the right
What are the rules about using a mobile phone while driving?	It is illegal to hold or use a mobile phone while driving — hands-free is permitted
