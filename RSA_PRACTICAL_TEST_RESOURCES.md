# RSA Practical Test Resources

These are the best **official** resources for the Irish practical driving test.

## Start With These

1. [Preparing for your test](https://www.rsa.ie/services/learner-drivers/the-driving-test/what-it-is)
   This is the current RSA page for the practical driving test. It says the test includes Rules of the Road questions, road signs, and practical driving assessment.

2. [Preparing for your driving test PDF](https://www.rsa.ie/docs/default-source/services/s1.5-driving-test/2885---rsa-driving-test-booklet_june24_3_web.pdf?sfvrsn=9fd22ffb_11)
   This is the best official booklet to actually study for the practical test.

3. [Checklist for your driving test PDF](https://www.rsa.ie/docs/default-source/services/s1.5-driving-test/checklist_for-dl_june24_3_web.pdf?sfvrsn=7d183f26_22)
   Use this to avoid failing on paperwork or vehicle requirements.

4. [Rules of the Road PDF](https://www.rsa.ie/docs/default-source/road-safety/rules-of-the-road.pdf)
   This is the official source for road signs, markings, lights, parking, right of way, and speed limits.

## What The RSA Explicitly Says To Do

From the RSA practical-test page:

- revise the Rules of the Road
- study the RSA test preparation booklet
- study the RSA driving test checklist
- use your Approved Driving Instructor for pre-test advice
- practise on all types of road except motorways

## Current Practical-Test Detail Worth Remembering

- From **9 March 2026**, the RSA says you must bring a **physical valid motor insurance cert** for the practical test.

## Best Use

Use the RSA material for:

- what is officially examined
- what documents and car requirements matter
- the official road-rule answers

Use the Ladybird 47 page for:
