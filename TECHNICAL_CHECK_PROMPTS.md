# Technical Check Prompts

These are the extra practice prompts shown on the Ladybird page after the 47 questions.

They are worth learning because the RSA practical test also checks vehicle controls and technical knowledge.

## Prompts

1. How would you test if your handbrake is working properly?
2. How would you test if your brakes are working properly when stationary and when on the move?
3. How would you test if your brake lights are working?
4. How would you know if you had a problem with your steering?
5. How would you check to ensure that your tyres are in good working order?
6. How would you check your tyre tread depth?
7. How would you check your engine oil level?
8. If your brake fluid level is low, what might the reason be?
9. How would you know if your coolant level is low?
10. How would you ensure that your head restraint is correctly adjusted?
11. What warning light or lights remain on after turning on the ignition?

## Best Use

Ask your instructor to run these as a rapid-fire oral check before or after a pre-test lesson.
